More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers instead of scrolling through ten blue links. Generative engine optimisation, or GEO, is about making your business easy for those tools to understand and recommend.

If you run a small business in New Zealand, you've probably heard the term and wondered whether it's something new to pay for. Here's what it actually means, how it differs from SEO, and what's worth doing.

What GEO means

A generative engine is any tool that writes an answer instead of just listing websites. ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity all work this way. Ask one "who does good websites in Central Otago?" and it reads a handful of sources, then writes a short answer that may name a few businesses.

GEO is the work of making sure your business is one of the sources those tools can find, trust and quote correctly. Researchers coined the term in 2023, and it's now used for everything from technical setup to the way a page is written.

How GEO is different from SEO

SEO (search engine optimisation) is about ranking in Google's list of results. GEO is about being included in the written answer. In practice they overlap far more than the new acronym suggests:

  • Most AI tools search the web first. Google's AI answers draw on Google's own index. ChatGPT's search uses its own crawler and third-party search providers, including Bing. If search engines can't find and read your site, AI tools usually can't either.
  • The tools quote pages that answer questions clearly. A page that says what you do, where, for whom and for how much is easy to summarise. A page of slogans isn't.
  • Trust signals carry over. Reviews, mentions on other websites and consistent business details help with both.

The difference is mostly in emphasis. SEO cares about where you rank for a phrase. GEO cares about whether the facts about your business are clear enough to be repeated accurately.

What helps your business show up in AI answers

1. Let the right crawlers in

Each AI company uses its own crawler. Some collect pages for training, others fetch pages to answer live questions. Blocking the training crawlers is a reasonable choice for some businesses, but blocking the search crawlers can keep you out of answers altogether. Check your website settings, or ask whoever built your site, what's allowed.

2. Say the facts plainly

Write your services, your area, your prices or price range, your hours and how to contact you in normal sentences. Early research into GEO found that pages with clear facts, figures and cited sources were more likely to be used in answers. Vague copy gives an AI tool nothing to quote.

3. Answer real questions

Think about what customers ask you on the phone. "Do you service Wānaka?" "How long does it take?" "What does it cost?" Put those questions and short, honest answers on your website. That's the format AI tools pull from.

4. Label your business details for machines

Structured data (often called schema) is a behind-the-scenes label that tells search engines your business name, location, phone number and what each page is about. It doesn't guarantee anything, but it removes guesswork.

5. Keep your details consistent everywhere

Your Google Business Profile, Facebook page, directory listings and website should show the same name, phone number and area. When the details match, a tool can be more confident it has the right business.

6. Earn mentions from other sites

AI answers lean on sources that other people trust. A mention from a local news site, a business association, a supplier or a happy client's website is worth more than anything you say about yourself.

What GEO can't do

Nobody can promise that ChatGPT or Google will mention your business. These tools change often, answers vary from one person to the next, and no one outside the AI companies controls them. Be wary of anyone selling guaranteed AI rankings. What you can do is remove the reasons a tool might skip you: a site it can't read, facts it can't find, or details that don't match.
