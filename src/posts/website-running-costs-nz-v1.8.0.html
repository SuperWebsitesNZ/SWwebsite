Building a website is a one-off cost. Running it is the part that keeps going. Here's what a small business website costs each month in New Zealand, and which costs you can avoid.

The short answer: anywhere from almost nothing to a few hundred dollars a month, depending on how the site is built and who hosts it. Most of the difference comes down to whether you're paying a subscription.

The costs every website has

Your domain name

Every website needs a domain, such as yourbusiness.co.nz. It renews every year, usually somewhere between NZ$20 and NZ$65 depending on the registrar. That works out to a few dollars a month. Make sure the domain is registered in your name, not your web designer's.

A security certificate

The padlock in the browser bar. It used to be a paid extra, but good hosting now includes it free. If someone is charging you for it every year, ask why.

The costs that depend on your setup

Hosting

Hosting is what keeps your site online. Some providers charge NZ$40 to NZ$150 a month. A simple, well-built small business site can be hosted for free, as long as it doesn't need a database or a shop running behind it.

Website builder subscriptions

Wix, Squarespace and similar builders bundle hosting, the editor and some features into a monthly plan. As a guide, Squarespace's plans were listed from NZ$21.85 to NZ$125.35 a month including GST, paid annually, in October 2026. Paying monthly costs more. The fee never stops, and if you leave, you usually have to rebuild the site somewhere else.

Care or maintenance plans

Many agencies sell a monthly plan for updates, backups and support, commonly NZ$40 to NZ$100 a month. On some plans the site goes offline if you stop paying. A care plan can be worth it if you change your site every week. If you change it a few times a year, paying per change is usually cheaper.

Plugins and add-ons

Sites built on WordPress often rely on paid plugins for forms, security or page building, each with its own yearly fee. They're easy to forget until the renewal emails arrive.

Costs that aren't really the website

  • Business email. An address at your own domain usually costs a monthly fee per mailbox from the email provider. It's separate from your website, even if the same company sells both.
  • Booking and shop tools. Booking systems and online shops often have their own monthly fee or take a cut of each sale.
  • Advertising. Google or Meta ads are optional, and only cost what you choose to spend.
