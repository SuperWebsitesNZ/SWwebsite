No mysterious fine print

GOOD QUESTIONS.
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

No subscription? What's the catch?

You pay for the build. There is no required ongoing website subscription with us. Domain renewals and any optional paid tools or extra hosting are separate and explained upfront.

Do I really own the website?

Yes. Your website code, domain and hosting account are yours. You choose who gets access, including anyone you want to work with later.

What if I need changes later?

Basic text or image changes are NZ$50 + GST. Larger redesigns or changes are quoted. A maintenance plan is not required to keep your site live.

Can I keep my current domain?

Yes. We review your domain, website and email setup before agreeing on any changes needed for launch.

What about bookings, shops or other tools?

We scope those individually. Existing tools may work well; some features need a larger build. Any third-party subscriptions or usage costs are explained separately.

How much does a website cost?

A basic website is NZ$1,500 + GST. We agree on the scope before building. Simple text or image updates are NZ$50 + GST; larger redesigns or changes are quoted. Domain renewals and optional paid services are separate.
