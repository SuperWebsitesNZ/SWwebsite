A good business website doesn’t need to be huge. It needs to make the next step easy.

Someone arrives on your site with a few simple questions: What do you do? Is it right for me? How do I get in touch? Answer those clearly and a small website can do a big job.

Start with what customers need

Explain your main service in everyday language. Use real examples or photos where you can, and give people a clear way to contact you. A clever headline is fun, but visitors still need to understand what you offer.

Give each page a purpose

A focused one-page site can suit a straightforward business. If you offer several different services, separate pages can give each one room to breathe. The right size depends on what your customers need to find.

Keep ownership simple

Before choosing a website provider, ask who owns the site, who controls the domain and what happens if you stop paying a monthly plan. Knowing those answers upfront makes it easier to plan for the years ahead.
