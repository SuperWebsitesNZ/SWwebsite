SEO and GEO, in plain English

SEO (search engine optimisation) is making your website easy for Google to find, read and show to the right people. For a small business, that mostly means: does your site come up when someone nearby searches for what you do?

GEO (generative engine optimisation) is the same idea for AI search. More people now ask ChatGPT, Google's AI answers or Perplexity things like "who builds decks in Cromwell?" These tools read websites and summarise them. If your site is clear and well labelled, they can understand your business and describe it correctly.

The good news is that both reward the same things: a fast site, clear answers, and business details that are consistent everywhere.

What nobody can honestly promise

No web designer can guarantee you the top spot on Google or a mention in ChatGPT. Anyone who does is guessing. Where you rank also depends on your competition, your reviews and how long your site has been around. New sites usually take weeks to months to settle into their positions.

What we can promise is a site that Google and AI tools can read properly from day one, with nothing holding it back.
