Straight answers.

What's the catch?

There isn't a monthly one. You pay for the build, and your standard website is hosted free, in an account in your name. Your domain renewal is paid to your registrar, not us. Any optional paid tools or extra hosting are scoped and explained before you commit.

Do I really own the website?

Yes. Your website files, domain and hosting account are yours. You hold the keys and choose who gets access, including any developer you want to work with later.

What happens if you two disappear?

Your site keeps running. The website files, hosting account and domain are all yours, so any web developer can take over without asking us for anything.

Can I edit the site myself?

Not with a drag-and-drop editor in our standard setup. We hand over all the website files, so you or another developer can edit them. If you prefer us to do it, quick text or image updates taking up to 30 minutes are NZ$50 + GST. A maintenance plan isn't required to keep your site live.

What if I need changes later?

Quick website updates taking up to 30 minutes are NZ$50 + GST. Larger redesigns or changes receive a fixed quote before we start. Ask when you need something changed; there's no required ongoing plan.

Can I keep my current domain and email?

Yes. We review your domain, website and email setup before agreeing on any changes needed for launch, and check that your email keeps working when the new site goes live.

What about bookings or an online shop?

Integrating an existing booking platform starts from NZ$150 + GST. Online shops and other custom functionality receive a fixed quote. Some features need a larger build. Booking and shop tools can have their own monthly fees or usage costs, which we explain before you decide.

Who writes the website content?

We do. Copywriting is included in both website packages. You provide the basic information about your business, services and customers, and we write the website content from that.

How much does a website cost?

A one-page website is NZ$1,500 + GST. A website with up to five pages is NZ$2,950 + GST. Both include design and copywriting from your basic business information. The five-page package also includes Google Business Profile setup and a photo gallery. Quick updates taking up to 30 minutes are NZ$50 + GST; custom work is fixed quoted.
