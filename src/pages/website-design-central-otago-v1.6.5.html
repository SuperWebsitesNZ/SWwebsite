Built for how Central Otago works

Business here runs to the seasons. Orchards, vineyards, cafés, trades, accommodation and tourism operators all get busy at different times of the year, and a website needs to keep up. A few things matter more here than in a big city:

  • Being found on Google Maps. In smaller towns, people search for a business on their phone and pick from the map. Your website and your Google Business Profile need to tell the same story. Google Business Profile setup is included in our five-page package.
  • Clear seasonal information. Opening hours, availability and what's on change through the year. A quick update from us is NZ$50 + GST, so keeping it current doesn't need a monthly plan.
  • Phones first. Most local searches happen on a phone, often on the move. Every site we build is designed for small screens first.
  • Visitors and locals. Many Central Otago businesses serve both. Your site should make sense to someone driving through for the first time and to a regular who just wants your hours.
