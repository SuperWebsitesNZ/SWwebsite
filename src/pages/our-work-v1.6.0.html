Before Super Websites, Dave built websites for his own two Queenstown businesses. They're real sites with real customers, and they show the approach we now bring to every client: clear pages, words written for the customer, and the business owner holding the keys.

FanCast Productions

fancast.co.nz · Video production, Queenstown and Central Otago

The brief

FanCast Productions films events, weddings, corporate video, drone footage and live streams. It works in both Queenstown and Melbourne, and one website was trying to speak to both markets at once. The New Zealand business needed a site of its own that spoke clearly to Queenstown and Central Otago clients.

What the site does

  • A dedicated New Zealand website, with the Melbourne business kept on its own site
  • A separate page for each service, from event and wedding videography to drone, live streaming and corporate video, so each one can be found on Google
  • A video showreel on the homepage, because for a video company the work should do the talking
  • A portfolio of case studies, a client reviews page and a regularly updated blog
  • An enquiry form that sends each lead straight into the business's customer system
  • Business details that match the Google Business Profile exactly

CamerAcademy

cameracademy.com · Camera, filmmaking and content classes in Queenstown, Cromwell and Dunedin

The brief

CamerAcademy runs camera and content classes for adults and teens, plus training for businesses and photography tours. People needed to understand each course, pick a location and book, without a long email back and forth.

What the site does

  • A page for each course, with dates, times, prices and what you'll learn
  • Location pages for classes outside Queenstown, including Cromwell and Dunedin
  • Pay-now buttons with a location choice, so students can book and pay on the spot
  • Photography tours connected to the booking platform that already sells them
  • Google reviews on the pages where people make their decision
  • A blog with student stories and practical camera tips, updated regularly

What both sites have in common

  • Written for customers. Plain words about what's on offer, who it's for and what it costs.
  • Built for phones. Both are designed for small screens first.
  • Owned by the business. No website builder subscription sits behind either site.
